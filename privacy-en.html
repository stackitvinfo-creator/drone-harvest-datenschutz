2. Game and local data

The game saves progress, unlocks, credits, gameplay and display settings and control bindings on your device. It uses this data to resume your game and apply your settings. The game requires no separate developer account. On Android it attempts to sign in to Google Play Games. Local saving remains available if sign-in does not succeed. Cloud saves contain permanent progress. Runs in progress and device settings stay local. This permanent progress is backed up to the connected Play Games profile. The weekly rewards service processes the limited data described in section 7.

Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity Purchasing and Unity cloud diagnostics are disabled in this configuration. We run no advertising or usage analytics of our own in this test. Section 7 describes analytics and diagnostics from the Play Games Services SDK.

You can clear local app data in Android settings. This can remove local saves and settings. It does not automatically remove a Play Games cloud save, which can be restored when you sign in again. If you enable other device backups, their provider's terms apply.

3. Voluntary feedback and bug reports

Our support page links to Google Forms for feedback and bug reports. Depending on the build, the game also links to the forms. Use is voluntary.

We receive the information you choose to submit, such as your comments, ratings for enjoyment, controls and performance or a bug report with steps and the expected and actual result. You may also provide the game version, device model, Android version, frequency and an email address for replies. The forms do not automatically collect your Google email address, accept file uploads or display responses publicly. The game attaches no saves, images, identifiers or logs.

Google Forms stores responses in our Google account. When you open a form, Google also processes technical connection and usage data such as your IP address, browser information and, where relevant, cookies or account information. Google may process this data outside the EU or EEA. You can contact us directly by email instead. Please avoid sending passwords, confidential information or someone else’s private data. 6. Retention and deletion

We delete personal form responses and the corresponding support messages within six months after the matter closes, subject to legal retention duties or the protection of legal claims. We may keep fully anonymised bug reports and suggestions for development. You can clear local saves and recovery copies through the app data settings on your device. Google Play Games manages cloud saves and leaderboard entries. Clearing the app’s local data does not automatically delete Google's copy. Delete Play Games data for a particular game

The weekly rewards service deletes score records four weeks after the relevant weekly close. Claims, including uncollected awards, and their acknowledgement records expire three calendar months after that weekly close and are deleted automatically afterwards. After the final claim deadline, automatic cleanup also removes the pseudonymous eligibility identifiers for the weekly rewards test. These periods apply to the weekly rewards service. Items and in-game currency already awarded form part of local progress and, where enabled, the synchronised save. To request earlier deletion, email us with the subject “Drone Harvest – Data deletion”. Say whether the request concerns test registration, support, or weekly rewards. If we need to match records to you, we will explain what information we need to verify your request. Use Google’s deletion controls for separately held Google Play Games data.

7. Google Play

When you join a test, install the app or use Google Play and Google Play Games, Google processes account, device and usage data under its terms. Drone Harvest attempts to sign in to Google Play Games on launch. After sign-in it uses Saved Games to read and write a compressed backup in the connected Play Games profile. Data includes credits, technology unlocks, purchased in-game upgrades, NORA progress, aggregated game statistics and the last 64 run summaries at most. Runs in progress, gameplay and display settings and control bindings stay out of this cloud save.

According to Google, the Play Games Services SDK handles the gamertag and avatar as well as analytics and diagnostics to maintain and improve the service. Drone Harvest uses the game-specific player identifier for Play Games features. The app does not read your Google email address for these features. It saves the identifier and a checksum of the last synchronised profile on the device for sync. If saves conflict, the game makes local recovery copies and asks you which save to use. The game saves locally if Play Games sign-in fails.

Leaderboards by map

With a connected Play Games profile, the app sends a computed score for the map to Google Play Games after a ranked run. The value includes points, run duration and kills. Google maintains separate weekly and all-time leaderboards for each map. The app shows the ranking, result and Play Games gamertag. Other people may see these details depending on the visibility of your Play Games profile. You can change visibility in your Play Games profile. Google's retention applies to Play Games leaderboards. The weekly rewards retention periods in section 6 apply to the separate rewards service.

Weekly rewards in the closed test

The weekly rewards service compares public weekly scores verified through Google Play Games for enabled test profiles on each map. Rewards are game items and in-game currency. Rank for rewards is determined among the eligible test profiles with verified scores and may differ from the public leaderboard. When your profile is connected, the app can call the service in the background to verify a score or retrieve and acknowledge a reward.

If you opt into weekly prizes, the app sends your participation code with the Google authorisation code. The service stores only a hash and expiry time for the participation code. When redeemed, the service links the code to a pseudonymous Play Games identifier and automatically deletes expired invitations.

The app sends a short-lived Google authorisation code over HTTPS to the weekly rewards service hosted on Google Apps Script. It exchanges the code for a Google access token for the request. Depending on the request, the app also sends the map and score or a random installation identifier and the identifiers of rewards received. The service checks eligibility and prevents duplicate grants. It uses authorisation codes and access tokens only for the corresponding request. It stores weekly scores and reward records in Google Cloud Firestore under derived pseudonymous identifiers with the map, verified score and time or, for rewards, the rank, grant type and amount and acknowledgement status. Weekly rewards records use only the derived pseudonymous identifiers to associate records with a player. Service errors are reported to the operator without player names or results.

These services are provided by Google and process technical connection data. Google may process data outside the EU or EEA. Google’s Privacy Policy · Privacy information for Firebase

If you send feedback through Google Play and we receive it, we use it to run the test and improve the game. The app does not process payments itself in this test.
